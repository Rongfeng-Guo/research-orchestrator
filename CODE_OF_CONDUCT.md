# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We want Research Orchestrator to be a respectful, technically rigorous, and constructive project.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a healthy community:

- Giving actionable, evidence-based feedback
- Assuming good intent while being precise about technical concerns
- Respecting different experience levels and backgrounds
- Accepting correction without escalating conflict

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- Deliberate misrepresentation of results, benchmarks, or authorship
- Publishing private information without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject comments, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this code of conduct.

## Reporting

Report serious conduct concerns to `rongfengguo.cs@gmail.com`.

When possible, include links, screenshots, and a short factual timeline.
